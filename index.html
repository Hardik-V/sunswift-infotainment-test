Infotainment aptitude test

Background

Sunswift 8 (SR8) is a road-legal solar, hydrogen and battery car. It has two displays. The driver display is behind the steering wheel. The centre display is in the middle of the dashboard.

The infotainment team designs and builds the software for the two displays. This test is about the driver display.

The brief

The driver display is behind the steering wheel. It is 1920 × 720 pixels. The driver reads it in less than two seconds, often in sunlight.

We mark all four parts against these rules. The rules stay on the Brief tab.

  1. Speed is the most important item. Make it the biggest item. Put it in the centre, straight ahead of the driver.
  2. Use red only for a danger. Use amber only for a caution.
  3. Hydrogen is always orange. The battery is always white.
  4. Show each fact one time only.
  5. A warning shows the problem in words.For example: REAR LEFT 24 PSI, not only a symbol.
  1. The driver display does not chat or entertain.No messages, social media, music or video.
  2. The driver reads the display but does not touch it.No buttons, menus or scrolling.
  3. Items do not overlap or touch.
  4. The speedometer can show more than the true speed, but never less.

Part 1 · about 8 minutes

Rank the designs

Rank the five designs for the driver, from 1 (best) to 5 (worst). Use the brief.

Part 2 · about 12 minutes

Arrange the display

This is the busiest moment for the driver display. The car is at 90 km/h, navigation is on, and there are two warnings.

Put each item that follows the brief on the display. Leave off each item that breaks the brief. Then arrange the display so that the driver can read it in two seconds.

Drag items onto the display. Click an item on the display to change its size or to remove it.

Part 3 · about 10 minutes

Write two functions

Write each function yourself, or use AI. Some checks are hidden. They run after you submit, so make sure that your code follows each line of the spec.

speedText(reading)

Returns the text that the speedometer shows. reading.kmh is the true speed from the wheel sensors. reading.ageMs is the age of the reading in milliseconds.

  1. Show a whole number that is never less than the true speed.
  2. When the car reverses, the speed is negative. Show the speed without the minus sign.
  3. If the reading is older than 500 ms, show --. If the speed is missing or is not a number, show --.
  4. Show only the digits, or --. Do not show units or spaces.

    tyreWarning(kpa)

    Returns the warning text that the display shows. kpa has the four tyre pressures in kilopascals: fl, fr, rl and rr (front left, front right, rear left and rear right). If a sensor has failed, its value is null.

    1. Warn when a tyre is below 26 psi. 1 psi is 6.895 kPa.
    2. If more than one tyre is low, show only the lowest tyre.
    3. Use this format: the tyre name in capitals, the pressure in psi rounded to the nearest whole number, then PSI. For example: REAR LEFT 24 PSI. The tyre names are FRONT LEFT, FRONT RIGHT, REAR LEFT and REAR RIGHT.
    4. Ignore failed sensors.
    5. If no tyre is low, return an empty string.

      Part 4 · about 15 minutes

      Design the charging screen

      Design what the driver display shows while SR8 charges. Use any tool: Figma, Canva, PowerPoint, paper and a photo, or AI. A rough design is fine.

      The design must

      • Show the three energy sources: the battery from a plug, hydrogen from a pump and solar from the roof. Two or three sources can work at the same time.
      • Show how much each source adds now, and when the car is ready to drive.
      • Be easy to read from outside the car, through the side window.
      • Follow the brief.
